What it does

  • Reads a unified diff (diff -u or git diff) and lists files, hunks, and added and removed lines.
  • Checks every hunk header against its lines, the cause of "corrupt patch" and "malformed patch".
  • Applies the diff to a file you paste and says which hunk fails and which line differs, or shows the result.
  • Warns about CRLF in the diff and about blank lines where context lines should have a space.

How far to trust it

  • On 1,500 generated diffs (4,314 checks, 0 differences), applying a diff to its own original gave the new file, and on altered originals the outcome and result matched GNU patch 2.7.6 with --fuzz=0. Corrupted hunk counts were always caught, and the "corrupt patch" claim was confirmed by hand with both patch and git apply. Renames, binary patches, fuzz and whitespace options are not handled.
